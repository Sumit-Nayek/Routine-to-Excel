## Routine-to-Excel
Transform your academic timetable into a structured, actionable Excel sheet in seconds. This project automates the tedious, manual process of copying class schedules, freeing up valuable time for what truly matters in the new era of education.

### 🚀 Why This Matters: The NEP 2020 Connection
India's National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 isn't just a policy; it's a paradigm shift. It envisions an education system that is multidisciplinary, flexible, experiential, and deeply integrated with technology . The core of this transformation rests on giving students and educators more control over their learning paths and administrative burdens.

This tool is a small but powerful step towards that vision. Here’s how it directly aligns with and accelerates the NEP 2020 goals:

1. Powering the "Digital First" & Tech-Integrated Ecosystem
NEP 2020 heavily emphasizes digital learning and the integration of technology to enhance access and quality . Platforms like DIKSHA and PM eVidya are building the national digital infrastructure . This project complements this ecosystem by:

Automating a Core Administrative Task: It's a practical demonstration of how small automation scripts can solve everyday problems, reducing manual data entry and errors.

Creating Machine-Readable Data: By converting a routine into Excel, the data becomes ripe for further analysis, visualization, and integration with other tools.

2. Fostering Experiential, Skill-Based, and Applied Learning
The policy moves away from rote memorization towards experiential learning and skill development . It encourages students to engage in real-world problem-solving.

A Tool for Teachers and Students: This project can be used in project-based learning modules where students can:

Analyze their own schedules: Use the generated Excel sheet to categorize subjects, calculate study time, and plan effectively.

Learn Automation Basics: For computer science students, this is a tangible example of how Python can solve real problems, linking programming concepts to practical utility.

3. Bridging the Rural-Urban Divide Through Technology
NEP 2020 aims to reduce educational disparities and improve access for all, including in rural areas . This tool is a simple yet effective digital resource that can be adopted anywhere.

Low-Barrier Tech Solution: It does not require an expensive internet connection or sophisticated hardware to use once downloaded. It's a lightweight application that can help a teacher in a remote area organize their schedule just as effectively as one in a city.

### 🎯 Key Impact & Benefits
For Educators: Save hours of manual scheduling work. Focus on what you do best: teaching, mentoring, and developing new curricula.

For Students: Gain a structured, easily editable view of their timetable. Export it to calendars, or use it to track assignments and study sessions, promoting self-regulated learning.

For Administrators: Create consistency in timetable formatting for the entire institution, making it easier to manage and audit academic workflows.

### 🧰 Tech Stack & Setup
Language: Python
