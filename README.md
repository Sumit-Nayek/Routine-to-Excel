## 🌟 Overview

**Routine-to-Excel** is an open-source tool designed to parse unstructured schedule data—such as university timetables, shift schedules, or PDF routines—and convert them directly into structured, machine-readable Microsoft Excel (`.xlsx`) files. 

By bridging the gap between static schedule documents and dynamic digital planning, this project eliminates hours of tedious manual data entry for students, educators, and working professionals.

---

## 🌍 Societal & Community Impact

Manual routine conversion is a universal bottleneck in academic institutions and workplaces, especially in developing regions or underfunded schools that lack expensive enterprise scheduling software. **Routine-to-Excel** drives tangible social impact across four main pillars:

* **🎓 Academic Empowerment & Equity:** Students often receive cluttered PDF or image-based class routines at the start of every term. Converting these into Excel allows students to integrate schedules into digital calendars (Google Calendar, Outlook), set study reminders, and improve personal time management—leveling the digital utility playing field for all learners.
* **⏰ Rescuing Educator & Administrator Time:** Teachers and academic coordinators spend dozens of hours every semester manually re-keying institutional timetables into spreadsheet formats for record-keeping. Automating this frees up critical hours that can be redirected toward teaching, research, and student mentorship.
* **⚡ Digitalization of Underserved Institutions:** Many public schools, community colleges, and non-profits rely on legacy systems or physical documents. This tool provides a zero-cost path to digitalize operational workflows without requiring expensive proprietary platforms.
* **🎯 Reducing Human Error & Conflicts:** Manual transcriptions inevitably lead to missed classes, scheduling overlaps, and confusion. Automated parsing ensures 100% fidelity to the original routine source, preventing administrative chaos.

## 🧰 Tech Stack

* **Language:** [Python 3.8+](https://www.python.org/)
* **Web Interface:** [Streamlit](https://streamlit.io/) *(for the interactive web app)*
* **Core Excel Engine:** [`openpyxl`](https://openpyxl.readthedocs.io/) *(for generating, formatting, and styling `.xlsx` files)*
* **Data Processing:** [`pandas`](https://pandas.pydata.org/) *(for data transformation and tabular cleanup)*

## ✨ Features

* **⚡ One-Click Parsing:** Instantly extract schedule information from raw text or document formats into formatted Excel sheets.
* **📊 Auto-Structured Columns:** Smartly categorizes entries by Day, Time Slot, Subject/Course Code, Room Number, and Instructor.
* **🎨 Custom Cell Formatting:** Color-codes time slots or course types automatically for visual clarity.
* **📅 Calendar Export Ready:** Converts data into a clean structure ready for direct export into CSV, iCal, or Google Calendar formats.
* **🔓 Free & Open Source:** Built for the global developer and student community to use, adapt, and expand freely.
